Someone says "bruh". You say "bruh". Forever.

Bruh Forever watches your chat and answers every "bruh" with a "bruh" of its own. That's it. That's the addon. Then it grew some extras, because of course it did.

## What it does

- Says "bruh" back whenever someone else says "bruh" in **party, raid, guild or instance chat**. Whole word only, any capitalization.
- On by default in party, raid and guild. Instance chat is off until you turn it on.
- Never answers itself, waits 5 seconds between replies so two addons can't loop forever, and stays quiet if you started the bruh and someone just answered it.
- Optional sound on every bruh you hear, with **five bruhs to pick from** or a random one each time.
- Sound cooldown from 0 to 30 seconds. Set it to 0 and every bruh plays, even on top of each other.

## Bruh Mode

Type **!bruhmode** in chat. For the next 60 seconds, everyone with Bruh Forever turns sound on and drops the sound cooldown to 0. Then it all goes back to how it was. It never touches your saved settings, and you can switch it off with the **Allow Bruh Mode** checkbox if your group has had enough.

## Bruh counter

- `/bruh stats` shows bruhs heard, bruhs said and your **longest bruh chain** in each channel.
- `/bruh reset` clears it.
- Pause everything in raids or in Mythic+ if you'd rather not have the addon talking mid-pull.

## Settings

Open **Options > AddOns > Bruh Forever** for checkboxes, the sound picker and the cooldown slider. Everything is also a slash command:

- `/bruh <party|raid|instance|guild> <on|off>`
- `/bruh sound <on|off>`
- `/bruh soundpick <random|1-5>`
- `/bruh soundcooldown <seconds>`
- `/bruh bruhmode <on|off>`
- `/bruh pauseraid <on|off>` and `/bruh pausemythic <on|off>`
- `/bruh stats` and `/bruh reset`

## Works on

Retail, MoP Classic, Cataclysm Classic, Wrath Classic, TBC Classic, Classic Era and Forever.

## Good to know

- Blizzard doesn't let addons talk in /say, /yell or channels outside a keypress, so those aren't supported.
- On Retail, Blizzard hides chat from addons during boss encounters and Mythic+. Bruh Forever can't read or answer a bruh then.
- Found a bug or want a feature? Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Andy-Love/BruhForever/issues).
